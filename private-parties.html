FAQ

Good questions

Anything else? Call us on 083 800 1933. We're always happy to talk space.

How much space do we need?

Enough floor space and headroom for the dome, like a community hall, function room or large indoor space. Tell us about your venue and we'll confirm it's a fit.

What ages is it for?

All of them. We pitch every show to the group in front of us, from little ones to adults.

Can you come to our house?

If you have a large enough room, or a level garden in good weather, possibly. Send us the details and we'll let you know.

How do I book?

Call us on 083 800 1933 or send the form below with the date, venue and number of guests.
