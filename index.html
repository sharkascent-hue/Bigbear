About us

A planetarium that comes to you

Big Bear Planetariums is a mobile planetarium based in Ireland. We arrive, inflate our dome in your school hall, gym, library or field, and turn it into a window on the night sky. No bus, no permission slips, no long trip.

  • Immersive shows projected across the full dome overhead
  • Sets up indoors in halls and libraries, or outdoors at festivals
  • Content tailored to every age, from junior infants to adults
  • Two domes, so we can run bigger days and events

Our shows

All STEM subjects covered

Space is the doorway to every STEM subject. Our live, interactive shows weave science, technology, engineering and maths together, pitched to the age of the group in front of us.

S

Science

Physics, chemistry, biology & earth science
  • How stars are born, shine and die
  • The Sun, Moon, planets and the seasons
  • Gravity, light and the colours of the sky
  • Life on Earth and the search for life beyond it
T

Technology

The tools that let us explore
  • Telescopes, satellites and space probes
  • How we photograph galaxies billions of light-years away
  • Data, sensors and the technology behind every mission
  • The digital dome and how it is projected
E

Engineering

Designing things that work in space
  • Rockets, launchers and how they reach orbit
  • Space stations and the Moon landings
  • Rovers and robots exploring Mars
  • How astronauts live, work and stay safe
M

Maths

Numbers, patterns and shapes in the sky
  • Scale: how big is the Universe, really?
  • Distances, light-years and travel times
  • Orbits, circles, ellipses and geometry
  • Patterns, cycles and counting the stars

FAQ

Good questions

Anything else you'd like to know? Just ask. We're always happy to talk space.

Where do you set up the dome?

Anywhere with enough floor space and headroom: school halls, sports halls, libraries and community centres. Outdoors, we need a level grassy area at a festival or event. We'll confirm the exact space you need when you book.

Who are the shows suitable for?

Everyone! We run shows for primary and secondary schools, corporate groups, private parties, libraries and festivals. We tailor each show to the age and interests of the audience.

What do we need to provide?

Just the space and access to a standard power socket. We bring the dome, the projection equipment and everything else.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Ireland and travel to schools and events. Send us your location in the booking form and we'll let you know availability.

How do I book?

Call us on 083 800 1933, or fill in the form below with your date, venue and group size and we'll come back to you with availability and a quote.
